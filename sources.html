03 — Model outputs

2027, 2030, and 2035 are scenarios.

Not official forecasts

The future mix values are student-built base-case estimates based on the assumptions stated on this site. They should not be presented as EIA, PJM, Dominion, Maryland PSC, or government forecasts.

Hourly simulation

The 24-hour demand and dispatch curves are normalized teaching inputs, not actual hourly state metering. Their purpose is to demonstrate how dispatch logic changes over the day.
