03 — How to read it

What changes through the day?

Midday

Solar rises strongly. Storage may charge if low-cost generation exceeds immediate needs.

Evening

Solar falls while demand can stay high. Gas, hydro, storage discharge, and imports become more important.

Overnight

Demand falls, nuclear stays relatively steady, and some storage can recharge.

04 — Forecast assumptions

What the 2035 model assumes.

Virginia

Fast load growth; nuclear roughly steady in absolute output; strong solar growth; offshore wind added; coal near zero; natural gas remains important for balancing and reliability.

Maryland

Slower demand growth; Calvert Cliffs remains online in the base case; coal declines; solar and wind grow; imports remain important.

If Calvert Cliffs does not continue operating past its current license period, Maryland's 2035 mix could shift much more heavily toward gas, imports, or additional renewables.
