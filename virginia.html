What drives the forecast

The key issue.

Virginia's biggest modeling challenge is that total electricity demand may grow very quickly. That means a source can lose percentage share while still producing more electricity in absolute TWh. The base case assumes nuclear output stays roughly steady, solar and wind expand, coal approaches zero, and gas remains important for reliability and load growth.
