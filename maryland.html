What drives the forecast

The key issue.

Maryland's biggest modeling challenge is nuclear uncertainty. The base case assumes Calvert Cliffs continues operating through 2035. If that assumption fails, Maryland would likely need much more natural gas, imports, renewable generation, or some combination of them to replace the lost nuclear output.
